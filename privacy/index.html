Privacy Policy

Clickoz is built around fast browser utilities. The default product principle is simple: your tool input should stay on your device whenever the tool can run locally.

What Clickoz processes

Clickoz pages are designed as client-side tools for SEO, writing, developer and creator workflows. You can open a tool, paste input, generate output and copy results without creating an account.

We do not ask for registration, passwords or billing details inside the current public tool experience.

Browser-only tool input

Most Clickoz tools run in your browser. Text, URLs, JSON, Base64 strings, UTM values and similar inputs are processed locally by JavaScript on your device. They are not intentionally uploaded to Clickoz servers by the tool logic.

Do not paste secrets, credentials, private keys or production tokens into any online tool unless you are comfortable handling that data in your browser environment.

Cookies and local storage

Clickoz uses small browser-side preferences to keep the interface consistent and fast on your device. These choices are not accounts, uploads or advertising profiles.

Essential only keeps the privacy choice plus interface preferences such as theme color and language, so the interface does not reset while you move between tools, guides and updates. Allow smart cache also lets the browser prepare likely internal Clickoz pages so tools can open faster, and enables translation only when you request another language. No extras turns off smart page caching and optional translation; Clickoz only remembers that choice so the consent panel does not keep appearing.

Browser cache means your browser may reuse static Clickoz files such as styles, scripts and icons instead of downloading them again on every page. It does not give Clickoz access to files on your device, and it does not store the private text you paste into tools.

  • clickoz_consent: remembers your privacy choice.
  • clickoz_accent: remembers your selected theme color.
  • clickoz_language: remembers the language selector when you choose one.
  • Browser storage: may be used for temporary UI state and faster internal navigation, not account tracking.

Optional third-party services

If you request translation, Clickoz may contact Google Translate to translate visible page text. Google may process technical data under its own terms and privacy policies.

If you choose essential-only or no extras and do not request translation, Clickoz does not intentionally call the translation service.

Security and infrastructure logs

Like most websites, hosting and security infrastructure may process basic request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp. This helps detect abuse, debug availability issues and improve performance.

Clickoz also applies client-side hardening for unsafe links and external tabs, and the public pages include security-oriented metadata where compatible with the static CMS.

Your choices and contact

You can clear browser storage at any time from your browser settings. You can also use the cookie controls on the site to refuse optional third-party services.

Questions or removal requests can be sent through Contact. Include the page URL and the specific privacy question so it can be handled accurately.
