Tool usage notes

Clickoz tools are designed for practical workflows: paste, inspect, fix, copy and continue. Outputs should be reviewed before publishing, especially for SEO metadata, generated copy, encoded values and debugging results.

Privacy-first direction

The CMS prioritizes browser-side processing where possible. Optional third-party functionality such as Google Translate is tied to user consent and should remain clearly separated from essential tool behavior.

Public readiness checklist

  • Important pages have canonical URLs and indexable metadata.
  • Tools and guides are linked internally by workflow.
  • Legal pages explain privacy, terms and contact paths.
  • A custom 404 page helps users recover instead of leaving.
